# Asset Management

Concept | Definition|
--------|-----------|
Asset | Anything valuable to an organization that must be protected
Asset Inventory | A record of an organization<s assets
Asset Management |the process of tracking assets and the risks that affect them
Asset Classification | Categorizing assets based on their sensitivity and business value
Asset Owner | Person or entity responsible for an asset
Sensitivity | Level of protection required for an asset

# Types of Assets 

Type | Examples
-----|---------|
Hardware | Laptop, Desktop, Router, Smartphone, Server
Software | Operating System, Applications, Databases
Data | Customer records, Financial records, Emails
People | Employees, contractors, Customers
Facilities | Office buildings, Data centers

# Data Classification Levels

Classification | Description
---------------|------------|
Public | Information accessible to anyone
Internal-only | Information intended for internal use
Confidential |Information accessible only to authorized users
Restricted | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access controls

Examples of Restricted Information

Benefit | Description
--------|------------
Visibility | Helps identify  organizational assets
Risk identification| Helps identify threats and vulnerabilities
Classification | Helps apply appropriate protections
Compliance |Supports regulatory requirements
Security Planning | Helps prioritize security efforts
