# PASTA Stages

PASTA stands for Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis.

| Stage | Name |Objective |
|---:|---|---|
| I | Define business and security objectives | Understand business goals and security requirements |
| II | Define technical scope | Identify technologies, systems, APIs, and infrastructure |
| III | Decompose the application | Analyse components, data flows, and trust boundaries |
| IV | Threat analysis | Identify threat actors and threat events |
| V | Vulnerability analysis | Identify exploitable weaknesses |
| VI | Attack modelling | Map plausible attack paths |
| VII | Risk and impact analysis | Evaluate business impact and prioritize controls |

## Example Controls
- MFA
- Prepared statements
- Input validation
- Encryption
- RBAC
- Least privilege
- Secure session management
- Logging and monitoring
- Vulnerability assessment
- Penetration testing


## PASTA Outcome

PASTA connects technical attack scenarios with business consequences and risk-treatment priorities.
