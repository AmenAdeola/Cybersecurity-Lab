# PASTA Stages

Stages | Name | Objective|
-------|------|----------|
I | Define Business and Security Objectives | Understand business goals and security requirements
II |Define the Technical Scope | Identify technologies, systems, APIs, databases, and infrastructure
III| Decompose the Application | Understand data flows, processes, and trust boundaries
IV | Threat Analysis | Identify potential threats and threat actors
V | Vulnerability Analysis | Identify weaknesses that could be exploited
VI | Attack modeling| MAP assets, threats, and vulnerabilities into attack paths
VII | Risk Analysis and Impact| Identify controls and reduce risk
