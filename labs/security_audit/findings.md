# Security Audit Findings

## Finding 1- Access Control Weaknesses

### Observation

All employees currently have access to internally stored data.

### Risk

Unauthorized access to customer or business information.

Implement least privilege access controls and role-based access management.


-------

## Finding 2- Lack of Encryption

### Obeservation

Sensitive data is not encryted

### Risk

Exposure of customer and payment information if systems are compromised.


### Recommendation

Implement encryption for sensitive information at rest and in transit

--------

## Finding 3- Absence of Backup Strategy

### Observation

No formal backup procedures exist.


### Risk

Permanent loss of data during incidents such as ransomware attacks or hardware failures.

### Recommendation

Implement automated, tested, and secure backup procedures.

--------

## Finding 4- No disaster Recovery Plan

### Observation 

No disaster recovery strategy has been documented.

### Risk 

Extended downtime and business disruption during emergencies>

### Recommendation 

Develop and regularly test a disaster recovery plan

--------

## Finding 5- No Intrusion Detection System

### Observation

No IDS solution is currently deployed.

### Risk

Delayed detection of malicious activity. 

### Recommendation

Implement intrusion detection and security. monitoring capabilities.
