# Risk Assessment and Risk Register

## Overview 

This project demonstrates the process of identifying, evaluating, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks using a risk register.

A risk assessment was performed for a fictional commercial bank by analyzing threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, severity, and overall risk priority. The activity followed common risk management practices and concepts introduced in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).


## Skills Demonstrated

-  Risk Assessment
-  Risk Management
-  Risk Register Analysis
-  Asset Protection
-  Threat Identification
-  Vulnerability Analysis
-  Likelihood and Severity Scoring
-  Risk Prioritization
-  NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

## Risk Formula

Priority Score = Likelihood X Severity

## Outcome

This project demonstrated how organizations evaluate and prioritize cyber security risks to focus security resources on the most significant threats.
