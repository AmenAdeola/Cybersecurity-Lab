# Risk Register Analysis

## Objective

Perform a risk assessment for a commercial bank and prioritize potential risks affecting the bank's funds.

------------

## Operational Environment

The organization is a commercial bank located in a coastal area with low crime rates

Key characteristics include:
-  100 on-premise employee
-  20 remote employees
-  2,000 individual customer accounts
-  Strict financial regulations
-  Dependence on third-party organizations and suppliers

----------

## Risk Assessment Summary 

Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Priority|
-----|------------|----------|---------|
Business Email Compromise| 2|2\4
Compromised User Database |2|3|6
Financial Records Leak |3|3|9
Theft |1|3|3
Supply Chain Disruption|1|2|2

-----

## Risk Analysis

### Business Email Compromise

Description: An employee is tricked into sharing confidential information.

Assessment:

-  Moderate likelihood
-  Moderate impact

Priority Score: 4
-----

### Compromised User Database
Description: Customer data is poorly encrypted.

Assessment:

-  Moderate likelihood
-  Hight impact
Priority Score: 6

Potential consequences:
-  Unauthorized disclosure of customer information
-  Regulatory penalties
-  Loss of customer trust
----------

### Financial Records Leak
Description; A backup database server is publicly accessible.

Assessment: 
-  High likelihood
-  High impact

Priority Score: 9

Potential consequences:
-  Exposure of financial data
-  Regulatory fines
-  Reputational damage
-  Loss of customers
-------------

### Theft
Description: The bank's safe is left unlocked.

Assessment:
-  Low likelihood
-  High impact

Priority Score: 3

Reasoning: Although theft could have a significant financial impact, the likelihood is reduced because the bank operates in an area with low crime rates

----------

### Supply Chain Disruption
Description: Natural disasters delay deliveries.

Assessment:
-  Low. likelihood
-  Moderate impact

Priority Score: 2

Reasoning: The coastal location increases exposure to hurricanes and severe weather events, but such disruption occur infrequently

--------

## Notes

Doing business with external organizations increases the opportunities for sensitive information to be exposed or compromised.
Risks involving customer data and financial records require the highest priority because they could significantly impact operations, reputation, regulatory compliance, and customer trust. Physical theft remains important but is less likely due to the bank's low-crime environment.

---------

## Key takeaways

-  Risk assessments help organizations prioritize security resources
-  Not all risks require the same response effort.
-  Highly sensitive data often produces higher severity scores.
-  Risk registers provide visibility into organizational risks.
-  Likelihood and severity are combined to determine overall priority.
