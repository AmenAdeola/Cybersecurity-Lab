# Attack Surface and Attack Vectors  

## Objective  

Analyze the information and risks associated with a suspicious USB drive and recommend controls. 

# Contents

The USB drive contains a mixture of personal and work-related information such as such as family photos, travel plans, budgeting information, employee schedules, hiring documents, and other records. Some of these files may contain personally identifiable information PII and sensitive organizational data that  could be useful to an attacker


# Attacker Mindset

An attacker could use the information stored on the USB drive to perform social engineering attacks against Jorge or the hospital.Personal details, travel plans, employee schedules, and hiring information could be used to craft convincing phishing messages or impersonate trusted individuals. The USB drive itself could also be used as bait to introduce malicious software into the hospital network.


# Risk analysis

Potential consequences include malware infection, ransomware, credential theft, unauthorized access, data theft, and follow-on social engineering. 

# Security Controls

Control Type | Example|
-------------|--------|
Technical | Endpoint protection, antivirus, USB, device restrictions
Operational | Security awareness training
Administrative | Acceptable use policies/USB/device-control policies
Preventive | Disable unauthorized USB devices/Analyze unknown devices in an isolated environment
Detective | Endpoint monitoring
Corrective | Incident response procedures

Organization can reduce the risk of USB baiting attacks through technical, operational, and managerial controls. 
Employees should receive security awareness training that teaches them not to connect unknown USB devices to organization systems. 
Endpoint protection tools, device control policies, and antivirus software can help block malicious USB devices.
Additionally, organizations can restrict USB usage, implement least privilege principles, and require suspicious devices to be analyzed in an isolated virtual environment before being accessed.
