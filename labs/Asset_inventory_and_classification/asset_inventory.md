# Asset Inventory and Classification

## Objective

Identify organizational assets and classify them according to their business value, ownership, location, network access requirements, and sensitivity level.

## Asset Inventory

------------------------
 ## Asset Classification

 ### Restricted

 Access should be limited on a need-to-know basis
 
 Examples:

 -  Desktop

Potential impact:
-  Exposure of private information
-  Unauthorized access to personal files
 --------------

 ### Confidential

 Access should be restricted to authorized users .

 Examples:

 -  Network router
 -  External Hard Drive

Potential impact:
-  Network compromise
-  Disclosure of stored information
----------------------------------

### Internal-only

Used internally but contains limited sensitive information.

Examples:

-  Guest Smartphone
-  Streaming Media Player
-  Portable Game Console

Potential impact:
-  Unauthorized device access
-  Privacy concerns

-----------------

## Key takeways

-  Every connected device should be considered an asset
-  Asset inventories help organizations identify what must be protected.
-  Asset classification supports risk management and access control decisions.
-  Sensitivity measures required to protect and asset.
